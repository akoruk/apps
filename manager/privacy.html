Privacy Policy

For Zip Extractor — archive opener & file manager. Last updated 30 August 2026.

This app is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your documents and files stay on your device, and there is no account to create.

What stays on your device

Everything you open, extract or organise is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's own storage:

  • archives you open and everything extracted from them
  • files and folders you import, and anything extracted from an archive
  • passwords you type to open or protect an archive or PDF — these are used for that one operation and are not stored or transmitted

None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

What is collected

The app uses Google Firebase for three things:

  • Analytics — anonymous usage events (for example, which screens are opened and which features are used), together with the device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate region derived from your IP address, and a random app instance identifier. This is used to understand how the app is used and what to fix next. It is not linked to your name or to any account.
  • Remote Config — lets settings be changed without a new app release. It fetches configuration; it does not send your content.
  • Cloud Messaging — only if you allow notifications. It uses a push token issued by Apple for your device.

Google acts as our processor for this data. See Google's Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

You can stop analytics collection at any time in iOS Settings by turning off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or by turning off notifications for the app to stop push messages.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details. To confirm whether a subscription is active, the app sends the purchase receipt issued by Apple to Apple's servers for validation, and receives back only the subscription status and expiry date.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos — to import pictures you pick, and to save extracted photos and videos back to your library when you ask for it.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to unlock the app when you switch App Lock on. Authentication is performed by iOS; the app is told only whether it succeeded, and never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications — optional, for occasional app messages.

Each permission is requested only when you first use the feature that needs it, and the app works without the ones you decline.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email list.
  • No advertising SDK and no ads.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use your documents to train anything.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because there is no account, we hold nothing that identifies you personally. Deleting the app removes the content stored on your device and resets the random analytics identifier. If you have a question about your data, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
